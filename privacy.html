Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026.

Who uses it. Studio Uploader is used only by the owner of the Tragic, Lockdown Cook and Saint Creators YouTube channels, to manage those channels. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses. When the channel owner signs in with Google, it can upload and schedule videos on that owner's channel, edit their titles and descriptions, and read the channel's statistics and YouTube Analytics. It accesses no other account.

What it stores. The authorization tokens are kept on the channel owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

What it does not do. It collects no personal data about viewers or any other person, shows no advertising, and does not use Google user data to train AI models.

Google API Services. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
