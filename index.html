Studio Uploader

Studio Uploader is a private tool used by one small animation studio. It uploads the studio's own Blender-animated videos to the studio's own YouTube channels (Tragic, Lockdown Cook and Saint Creators), schedules them, and reads those channels' statistics and analytics so the studio can see how its videos perform.

It is not offered to the public, and only the channels' owner signs in to it.
